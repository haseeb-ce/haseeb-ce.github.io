### Hey, I'm haseeb.ce 👋 AKA IT4CH1

I'm a cybersecurity researcher and student, passionate about breaking things to understand how they work and how to defend them.

This blog is where I document my writeups from CTF competitions, TryHackMe, HackTheBox, and other security challenges I come across. I enjoy exploring all areas of offensive security, including:

- Web Exploitation
- Network Penetration Testing
- Active Directory & Red Teaming
- Reverse Engineering
- Cryptography
- Privilege Escalation

Whether it's a quick CTF flag or a full box walkthrough, I try to document my thought process clearly — not just the solution, but *how* I got there.

Feel free to explore the writeups, and reach out if you'd like to connect or collaborate.
